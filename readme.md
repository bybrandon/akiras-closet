# Akira's Closet 

The multiverse trembles as an ancient power reawakens. Somewhere beyond the fringes of reality, Victor Von Doom, better known as Dr. Doom, has broken through dimensional barriers with one goal: total dominion over all realities.

Having seized forbidden knowledge and absorbed fractured powers from fallen timelines, Doom is no longer just a tyrant of Latveria—he is a cosmic-level threat, one who bends both science and sorcery to his iron will.

Worlds have fallen. Universes have shattered. And Earth... Earth is next.

Our Leader, Strategist and recruiter Nick Fury has gone M.I.A but he left you, his capable right hand in charge of assembling a Team capable of stopping our impending DOOM. The world’s mightiest heroes have been scattered. Rogue agents, powerful mutants, forgotten warriors, and unstable anti-heroes walk the Earth. It’s up to you to find them, unite them, and forge them into something greater than the sum of their powers.

You must build a team that not only challenges Doom’s legions but survives the moral fractures and ideological conflicts that come with assembling such volatile personalities.

Because this isn’t just about saving the world...

This is about changing fate itself.

## 🎖️ Game Features
- Hero Roster – Choose from a growing list of Marvel characters
- Strategic Team Building
- User >-< Team Interaction
